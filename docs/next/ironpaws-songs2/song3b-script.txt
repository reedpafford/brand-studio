SCENE 1 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
Wide on the same bright game show studio: a glossy blue-and-gold stage ringed with marquee bulbs, the dog's gold host podium on the left, a second contestant podium on the right, a giant vertical one-to-ten meter glowing green to red at the side, and a huge curved mega screen behind.
The golden retriever stands at his host podium like a game show host, grinning, and the giant one-to-ten meter lights up behind him.
On the mega screen, a crowd of dog owners shrug and smile, unbothered.

SCENE 2 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
The meter flashes red and the marquee bulbs blink like an alarm.
The golden retriever fans out four blank scorecards in his paw and gives the camera a knowing look.

SCENE 3 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
Round one: the golden retriever at his podium slaps down a scorecard; close-up of the card showing a big number 4, and the meter jumps to 4.
The camera pans to the mega screen behind him and pushes in until the screen fills the frame.

SCENE 4 — EXT. PARK PATH — DAY
We come through the glowing edge of the game show mega screen, which falls away as we land in the real world: on a walk, the golden retriever grazes on the grass like a lawnmower, his belly gurgling, looking queasy, then munches even more to settle it.

SCENE 5 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
Round two: the golden retriever squints at the camera from his podium and slaps down a scorecard; close-up of the card showing a big number 6, and the meter jumps to 6.
The camera pans to the mega screen behind him and pushes in until the screen fills the frame.

SCENE 6 — INT. BEDROOM — MORNING
We come through the glowing edge of the game show mega screen, which falls away as we land in the real world: the golden retriever wakes up in bed with crusty, glued-shut eyes and pries one open with his paw.
Little grey specks drift out from the corners of his eyes.

SCENE 7 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
Round three: the golden retriever slaps down a scorecard; close-up of the card showing a big number 8, and the meter jumps to 8 with a flash.
The camera pans to the mega screen behind him and pushes in until the screen fills the frame.

SCENE 8 — INT. LIVING ROOM — DAY
We come through the glowing edge of the game show mega screen, which falls away as we land in the real world: the golden retriever licks his paws in a tongue-blur frenzy on the rug.
An angry red glow spreads under the fur of his paws and across his skin.

SCENE 9 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
Round four, the big one: a drumroll spotlight on the golden retriever, who slaps down a scorecard; close-up of the card showing a big number 9, and the meter shoots up to 9, flashing red.
The camera pans to the mega screen behind him and pushes in until the screen fills the frame.

SCENE 10 — INT. KITCHEN — DAY
We come through the glowing edge of the game show mega screen, which falls away as we land in the real world: his owner leans in to kiss the golden retriever; his breath puffs out a green stink cloud that wilts the houseplant on the counter, and she reels back.

SCENE 11 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
His owner stands at the contestant podium, looking sheepish and confused, as the meter glows red behind her.

SCENE 12 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
The golden retriever trots over to her podium and rests his head on her hand to comfort her.
He holds up a scorecard with a big heart on it, and she smiles.

SCENE 13 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
The studio lights dim to a single spotlight on the golden retriever, who leans toward the camera as if sharing a secret.

SCENE 14 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
The mega screen shows four glowing symptom icons — grass, an eye, a paw and a stink cloud — sliding together and merging into one glowing gut.
The golden retriever points up at the screen from his podium.

SCENE 15 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
A little conveyor belt rolls across the stage carrying dry kibble, a glass of tap water and artificial treats; each one gets a big red X on the mega screen.
Then a healthy golden glow sweeps across the mega screen.

SCENE 16 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
★ Peanut butter bag — the grand prize: the stage turntable spins to reveal the bag under a spotlight, with golden five-star shapes sparkling on the mega screen and his owner clapping at her podium.

SCENE 17 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
The show's doors slide open and three cute ingredient heroes march onto the stage: a green chlorella wave buddy who splashes, tiny probiotic buddies carrying bricks, and a spirulina hero who flexes.
The golden retriever cheers from his podium.

SCENE 18 — INSIDE THE DOG'S GUT — A CUTE ANIMATED WORLD
Through the mega screen into a cute animated world inside the dog's gut: the probiotic buddies set the last bricks, the green wave washes it clean and twenty little ingredient sparkles light it up a healthy gold.

SCENE 19 — INT. KITCHEN — DAY
We come through the glowing edge of the game show mega screen, which falls away as we land in the real world: ★ Peanut butter bag — his owner scoops the green powder from the bag into the golden retriever's bowl, and he digs in happily.

SCENE 20 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
Back on the set, weeks later: the golden retriever tosses his old scorecards over his shoulder one by one as the meter drops segment by segment from red down to green.

SCENE 21 — EXT. PARK — DAY, WEEKS LATER
We come through the glowing edge of the game show mega screen, which falls away as we land in the real world: the golden retriever trots right past the grass and runs happily beside his owner, clean paws, tail wagging.

SCENE 22 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
The meter glows all green; the golden retriever at his host podium gives the camera a wink, and confetti begins to fall.

SCENE 23 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
★ Peanut butter bag — the bag sits on the spinning turntable under a spotlight while the mega screen fills with a crowd of happy dogs and their owners.

SCENE 24 — INT. LIVING ROOM — GOLDEN HOUR
We come through the glowing edge of the game show mega screen, which falls away as we land in the real world: the golden retriever and his owner cuddle on the couch, both relaxed and happy; he's himself again.

★ Peanut butter bag — the golden retriever nudges the bag toward the camera with his nose from his podium as confetti falls; his owner smiles beside him.
