SCENE 1 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
Over the shoulder of the golden retriever at his gold host podium: the game show mega screen plays a replay of him on a walk grazing on grass like a cow, while two real cows in the next field stare at him.
The golden retriever turns back to the camera, cringes and slowly facepalms with one paw.

SCENE 2 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
Close-up on the golden retriever at his gold host podium: he slams a white scorecard down facing the camera, one big bold black number 4 on it, clearly readable, and holds it there; the giant meter behind him jumps to 4.

SCENE 3 — EXT. PARK PATH — DAY
We come through the glowing edge of the game show mega screen, which falls away as we land in the real world: on a walk, the golden retriever munches grass, his belly gurgles and he freezes, queasy and guilty, a blade of grass hanging from his mouth; his owner on the other end of the leash stares at him, baffled, palms up.

SCENE 4 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
Over the shoulder of the golden retriever at his gold host podium: the game show mega screen plays a replay of him waking up with his eyes crusted shut and walking straight into the bedroom door frame with a bonk.
The golden retriever winces and covers his eyes with both paws.

SCENE 5 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
Close-up on the golden retriever at his gold host podium: he slams a white scorecard down facing the camera, one big bold black number 6 on it, clearly readable, and holds it there; the giant meter behind him jumps to 6.

SCENE 6 — INT. BEDROOM — SUNNY MORNING
We come through the glowing edge of the game show mega screen, which falls away as we land in the real world: his owner sits on the bed and gently wipes the golden retriever's crusty eyes with a warm cloth; he leans into her hand, eyes closed, comforted.
Little grey specks drift away from the corners of his eyes.

SCENE 7 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
Over the shoulder of the golden retriever at his gold host podium: the game show mega screen plays a replay of him licking his paws so fast his tongue is a blur, the rug soaked around him.
The golden retriever slowly hides both paws behind the podium and looks away, all innocence.

SCENE 8 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
Close-up on the golden retriever at his gold host podium: he slams a white scorecard down facing the camera, one big bold black number 8 on it, clearly readable, and holds it there; the giant meter behind him jumps to 8, and a red alarm light flashes.

SCENE 9 — INT. LIVING ROOM — NIGHT
We come through the glowing edge of the game show mega screen, which falls away as we land in the real world: at 2 a.m. his owner, in pajamas, flicks on a lamp and catches the golden retriever mid-lick on the rug, tongue out, frozen like a burglar caught in the act.
An angry red glow spreads under the fur of his paws and across his skin.

SCENE 10 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
Over the shoulder of the golden retriever at his gold host podium: the game show mega screen plays a replay of his owner leaning in to kiss him and dramatically fainting backward onto the couch.
Under a drumroll spotlight, the golden retriever breathes into his paw, sniffs it, and his eyes water.

SCENE 11 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
Close-up on the golden retriever at his gold host podium: he slams a white scorecard down facing the camera, one big bold black number 9 on it, clearly readable, and holds it there; the giant meter behind him jumps to 9, red lights flash and a siren light spins.

SCENE 12 — INT. KITCHEN — DAY
We come through the glowing edge of the game show mega screen, which falls away as we land in the real world: the golden retriever pants happily and his breath puffs a green stink cloud that wilts the houseplant on the counter; his owner pinches her nose with one hand but pulls him into a hug with the other, laughing.

SCENE 13 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
His owner stands alone at the contestant podium under the red glow of the meter, presses her buzzer and gives a guilty, embarrassed shrug.
Then she covers her face with her hands, mortified, her shoulders slumping.

SCENE 14 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
The golden retriever trots across the stage to her podium, rests his head on her hand and holds up a scorecard with a big red heart on it.
Her eyes well up; she kneels and hugs him tight as warm golden light floods the stage.
