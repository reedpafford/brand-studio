SCENE 1 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
Wide on the same bright game show studio: a glossy blue-and-gold stage ringed with marquee bulbs, the dog's gold host podium on the left, a second contestant podium on the right, a giant vertical one-to-ten meter glowing green to red at the side, and a huge curved mega screen behind.
The golden retriever stands at his gold host podium, grinning, as the giant meter beside him lights up step by step from 1 at the bottom to 10 at the top.

SCENE 2 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
His owner stands at the contestant podium, relaxed, and waves it off with an easy smile and a shrug, as if it's all perfectly normal.

SCENE 3 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
The giant meter flashes red and an alarm light spins; his owner's smile drops and she stares up at it, shocked.

SCENE 4 — INT. GAME SHOW SET — BRIGHT
The golden retriever fans out four scorecards toward the camera in his paw and gives a slow, knowing look.
