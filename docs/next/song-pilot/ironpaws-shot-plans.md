# Iron Paws song ads: shot plans (for Reed's OK)

Both videos: 9:16, 480p, the House look (Pixar-style 3D), B-roll only (nobody sings or speaks on screen), the song as the only sound. Each clip below is one place and one continuous action (rule 29), 4–30 s. Times are the measured song times (the start of each line's first word); the final cut trims every clip to these times, on the beat.

**Cast (the same two in both videos, drawn once per video):**
- **The Frenchie:** a fawn French bulldog with big glossy eyes, bat ears, a stocky little body and a red bandana. Very expressive: dramatic sighs, side-eye, zoomies. He's the narrator and is in nearly every shot.
- **Her (the owner):** a warm woman in her mid-30s, messy bun, oversized cream knit sweater, kind and tired-looking at the start, glowing by the end.
- **Product:** the Iron Paws **peanut butter** bag, shown exactly (label to camera) at the find, the feeding, the proof and the end.
- **Home:** one cosy, lived-in home (a kitchen, a living room with a couch, a bedroom), the same in every scene.

The exaggeration follows the references: symptom close-ups played big and funny, a cute trip inside the gut, a mid-air bowl leap, and a warm, quiet payoff.
