## fomo

**How the brand talks:**

---
fomo is the thing that saves people, and it is only ever shown winning: losses happen on plain, unbranded apps or on a screen we don't see. Sell it in the flow of talk ("Stop picking. Copy the people who win."), never as an ad. Write the name FOMO in capitals in every spoken line (said FOH-moh). People find fomo in different ways: a friend shows them, they scroll past it on Instagram, they watch someone's gains, or they research how to win trades. Use the actual fomo UI, with any coin and numbers the story needs. Custom coin pages are fine with a big fomo header, the eyes logo and the character's face as the coin. Any loading screen is dark, with only the fomo eyes and logo. A chart may dip when the story needs it ("Don't sell. Diamond hands.") as long as it recovers and fomo wins. The payoff is a big balance on the rich Home screen, a different one in each video. Before the end line, ask the viewer one short yes/no question straight, with no set-up ("Would you take her back?"). Song videos skip it.
---

Other endings Reed approved: "Stop picking. Copy the winners on FOMO. Link's in my bio." / "Done getting rugpulled? Copy top traders on FOMO. Link's in my bio."):

## Iron Paws

**How the brand talks:**

---
Iron Paws is a dog gut-health greens powder. The dog tells it in first person, warm and a little funny, then tender at the recovery, with the refrain "That is my gut breaking down." The story: the dog's symptoms (eyes, paws, skin, ears, shedding) are the gut breaking down. The owner tries sprays and shampoos that don't work, then finds the gut fix. "We were good." Play the symptoms big and funny (a crust-glued eye, a fur blizzard), make the hidden cause visible (a glowing x-ray of the dog with hot spots), and keep the payoff quiet and warm (the apology or the hug). Show the peanut butter bag exactly, label to camera, at four moments: the find (on her phone), the feeding, the proof, and the end, with the dog nudging it to camera. Use only these claims, as written: human grade, vet formulated, probiotics, chlorella, spirulina, chicory root, burdock root, "plus 14 more ingredients", 3,000+ five-star Amazon reviews, 10,000+ owners last month. Songs: laid-back, breezy pop, a warm female vocal, about 100 BPM, strummed acoustic guitar; never pop-rap.
---

**End line:**

---
You just didn't know what the signs meant. But now you do. The link is below. Your pup has been waiting on you.
---
