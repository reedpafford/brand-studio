# Where the official prompting guidance fits our flow

This table maps each finding in [seedance-2.5.md](seedance-2.5.md) and [nano-banana.md](nano-banana.md) to our flow (Brief → Script → Breakdown → Storyboard → Clips; Quick clip). For each one it shows what we do today (prompts as of `c1f29b2`) and the exact change.

**Priority**
- **must**: an official source says our current way fails or is unsupported.
- **should**: an official recommendation we don't follow yet.
- **nice**: small, or needs a test first.

**Owners**
- Director: `server/app/prompts/` and `check.py`.
- Builder-Server: request building and the catalogue.

Reed's rules still apply to every row. Script and dialogue words never change. Enhance and Improve prompt only suggest camera, movement, reaction and where brand screens show. The only creative rules are "engaging" and "required brand screens featured exactly".

## Already right (keep)
- `[Image1]`/`[Video1]`/`[Audio1]` numbering in upload order ([SD §2](seedance-2.5.md#2-referencing-images-videos-and-audio)).
- Reference media rather than a first frame. Replicate can't combine the two ([SD intro](seedance-2.5.md)).
- One role per asset in the References line, e.g. `[Image3] is the fomo "Token chart up" screen` ([SD §2](seedance-2.5.md#2-referencing-images-videos-and-audio)).
- Brand screens shown from the reference and never described in words. This matches "don't re-describe an accurate reference" and "give exact text as an image" ([SD §2, §7](seedance-2.5.md#7-known-failure-modes)).
- Dialogue word for word in double quotes ([SD §3](seedance-2.5.md#3-dialogue-and-audio)).
- One camera move per shot, with a named subject ([SD §4](seedance-2.5.md#4-camera-language)).
- Audio references only with an image or video (`routes/clips.py`) ([SD §6](seedance-2.5.md#6-limits)).
- Catalogue Seedance limits: 30 images, 10 videos, 10 audio, 4–30 s, 480p/720p ([SD §6](seedance-2.5.md#6-limits)).
