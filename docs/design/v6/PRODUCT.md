## Platform

web

## Users
- **The owner (Reed).** Runs a small studio making short brand ads (fomo, Iron Paws, Resilia and others). Starts videos from an idea, a script or a reference reel, steers the director in a chat, answers its questions, approves spend past a video's budget, and owns the brands' saved characters, places, products and rules. Sees dollars.
- **Editors.** A small team making videos for the same brands, mostly from published scripts. They see credits and % of budget, never dollars. Their paid presses past an allowance or budget are held for the owner, never declined with "ask Reed".
- Both work at a desk (desktop first, 1440). Phones (390) must still work for checking progress, answering the director and watching the cut.

## Product Purpose
Make engaging brand videos fast, from a script or brief, with every brand's characters, screens and style loaded automatically. Each video idea is one chat with the site's director (Claude), which plans, casts, makes stills and clips, watches and listens to every take and the cut, redoes what's weak and delivers a cut with a report. Done means an editor on the live site makes a finished, on-brand video from brief to final cut without help (ops/MISSION.md).

## Positioning
The director works like Reed's own agent sessions: it talks the idea through, asks only when it truly doesn't know (with a card: pick saved, upload, reply, or generate options priced first), keeps working on everything else while it waits, and says plainly what it made, what it's unsure of and what it needs. Brands stay isolated; their saved assets fill each video's cast automatically.

## Operating Context
- Brand → free folders the user makes and names (a week, a style, a campaign; nestable; plus Unsorted) → video ideas, one director chat each.
- Four formats: Dialogue, Song, Narrated, Presenter (a host over B-roll). Styles: House (default), Disney Pixar, Claymation, Skeleton, Zack D, Dr. Pickle, UGC; Movie coming (6–9 min dramas, 100+ clips). B-roll look follows the style.
- Pipeline under the chat: treatment → plan (scenes, shots) → cast → stills → clip prompts → takes (model chosen per shot) → the director's watch → final cut (trim, re-time to the words, presenter over B-roll, swap B-roll, sound effects, music ducking). Captions are added outside the app.
- Money: 1 credit = $0.1028. Every paid press shows its "up to" price first. A video's budget is twice its 480p estimate; going past it waits for the owner.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- The script's spoken words never change. Brand must-shows (logo, app UI, product label) must read right.
- Nothing reaches clips without an approved sheet for every character in it.
- Looks generated on the spot for a video stay with that video; no "save to brand" for them for now. Only uploads and saved library items matter for reuse.
- No problem blocks without a one-click fix on the same screen. People never see raw ids.
- Light theme only for now. The established look (DECISIONS rule 33): Linear-style cool neutrals, one indigo action colour with white text for each screen's main press, Inter only, dense type, small corners, statuses as a dot plus words.

## Brand Commitments
- Plain language in everything Reed reads. Name characters, never ids.
- Editors see credits and %, never dollars; never "Ask Reed".

## Product Principles
1. The chat is the product: every step happens in, or is reached from, the video's conversation with the director.
2. The director asks only what it can't decide, and never stops the whole video for one answer.
3. Price before every paid press; the owner, not a wall, handles anything past budget.
4. Real pictures everywhere: a frame, a still or a take wherever there's media.
5. Scales from a 6-clip short to a 100+ clip drama without changing shape.
