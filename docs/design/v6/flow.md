# From an idea to a delivered cut

Each video idea is one chat with the director. The chat is where everything happens. The preview panel beside it shows the work itself: the plan, the cast, the clips and the cut.

## The path

1. **Pick the brand and the folder.** The rail shows the brand's folders. They're free folders you make and name (a week, a style, a campaign), and folders can sit inside folders. New ideas land in the open folder, or in Unsorted. *(S0-01, S1-04)*
2. **New video.** Paste a script, describe the video, pick a saved script, or give it a reel to adapt. Format and style are picture cards; each style plays a sample and says what its B-roll looks like. The brand's saved cast is filled in already. The button shows the most the run can charge. *(S2-02)*
3. **The treatment.** The director's first move is the treatment: the hook in the first 2 seconds, the story in one line, why it holds a viewer, and each beat. The owner presses "Make it · up to $X". Nothing is spent before that press. *(S3-02)*
4. **It works.** One live card shows the video as a strip of real frames, filling in scene by scene. Tap it for every step: what it's making, which model, and what it watched and redid. The Clips tab fills in alongside. *(S3-03, S3-04)*
5. **It asks only what it can't decide.** A card offers saved items from the brand first, then Upload, Reply, or Generate options (price on the button; what it generates stays with this video). It waits on that one point and keeps making everything else. A pinned bar above the message box and a dot in the tree show that something waits. *(S3-05, S4-01 to S4-04)*
6. **Money.** Inside the approved price, it just works. A step past the video's budget waits for the owner's OK in the chat and in Needs you. Nothing is charged while it waits. *(S3-06, S9-01)*
7. **It watches and listens.** Every take and the whole cut get watched as video plus sound. It redoes what's weak, inside the budget. *(S6-03, S6-04)*
8. **Delivered.** The cut plays in the preview panel. The delivery note reads like the agents' reports: what it made, what it watched and redid (with timecodes), what it's unsure of, what it needs from you, and the real total. Clips it changed stay outlined until you've watched them. *(S3-09)*
9. **Change it by talking.** "Shorter end", "swap the B-roll at 0:14". It says what goes out of date and the new price, and Cut 1 stays watchable until Cut 2 is ready. *(S3-12)*
10. **Or edit it yourself.** Open the cut editor to trim, re-time to the words, place the presenter over the B-roll, swap B-roll, add sound effects to a clip, or duck the music. The director's own edits are marked, each with Undo. Edits in the cut are free. *(S7-01 to S7-03)*
11. **Download.** The MP4, or every clip. Captions are added outside the app.

## What the owner sees

- Dollars everywhere: "$9.84 of up to $16.86".
- The start-of-run OK, and every request past a budget, in the chat and in **Needs you**, across every brand. *(S9-01)*
- Editors' held sends, to approve or decline. *(S9-01)*
- The Studio section: Needs you, In production, Usage, Team, Settings.

## What an editor sees

- Credits and % of the budget, never dollars: "96 of up to 164 credits". *(S3-10)*
- From a published script, inside the budget: it starts straight away. *(S2-06)*
- A pasted script (Freestyle), or a press past the budget or the weekly allowance: "Waiting for the owner's OK", with Withdraw. Never "ask Reed", and nothing charged while it waits. *(S2-04, S2-05, S3-07, S4-03)*
- A published script's words are set. Asked to change a line, the director offers what it can change instead. *(S3-14)*
- "My requests" in the rail instead of Studio. *(S0-01)*

## On a phone (390)

The same chat. **Chat | Preview** switch at the top, and open questions sit in a bar above the message box. The cut plays full width. The phone shows the list of edits and lets you ask the director for more; the full timeline is desktop only. *(P-01 to P-15)*
