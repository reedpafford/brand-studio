## Why it works

- Title caption 'Daddy said I'd fail' plants the payoff in frame 1, so every humiliation reads as setup for revenge.
- The hook breaks the house pattern: a single held wide with fast, cruel dialogue, ending on an unanswered question and a silent beat - the silence is the joke.
- Clear ticking clock ('You have 30 days'), said face to face in an extreme close-up profile two-shot, then echoed as V.O. at rock bottom.
- Failure is played as comedy (six match-cut shirts, one-word deadpan reactions, sleeping on bin bags, slapstick dive into a puddle), which keeps a sad stretch watchable.
- Lighting does the storytelling: night, rain and screen-glow for the low point; first daylight (bright blue sky) exactly on 'Let's work!'; cool high-key warehouse for success.
- The turn is sound-led and escalates: phone dings stack up while a slow push accelerates into a crash zoom into his pupil.
