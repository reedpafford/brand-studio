## Why it works

- The hook is a universal nightmare at peak stakes: a public proposal, a crowd filming, and a shouted 'Hell no!' in the first second.
- The rival is introduced by objects before faces - a gold watch, then a Rolls-Royce grille - so 'money over love' reads instantly without exposition.
- The wound is held, not told: one long push-in from a medium shot to a tear in his eye, then a soft-focus 'PREVIOUSLY...' memory of 'I'll never leave you' that makes the betrayal sting.
- The turn is purely visual: the crowd vanishes, the sun flares behind him and his face hardens in a held push-in - a clean 'decision' beat with no words.
- The grind montage is tiny (1.4 s) but legible: one locked-off frame flickering day/night says 'years of work' through match cuts. It is the exact slot where a brand can put its product screen.
- Mirrored staging makes the payoff land: Paris and a Rolls-Royce in both halves, but now he is in the bigger car, at eye level with her, and she is the one behind glass.
- Escalating flex beats inside the payoff - hypercar, smile, point to the sky, 'NATE' jet, 'NATE' plate, exhaust flames - each one a separate 1-3 s shot, joined by whips.
- The comeback is playful, not angry ('La la la, I can't hear you!'), which makes it shareable and quotable.
- Music carries the 14 s wordless middle and builds into the live-action tag, where a bass-heavy drop and 0.1-0.2 s cuts make the creator's real life feel like the sequel.
- The tag ends on an on-screen CTA ('CREATE VIDS IN BIO') that matches the caption CTA, turning the story's flex into a funnel.

## Method

faster-whisper 'small' (CPU, int8). The first full pass (16 words from 5.0 s) missed most lines, so it was re-run on short windows (0-2.2, 10.3-13, 12.8-16.2, 16-26, 26-39.4 s) and every line checked against the burned-in word-by-word captions. Whisper also hallucinated 'The blue...' (15.1-16.2) and 'Thank you for watching!' (23-26) over music; both dropped. Auto cut list (frame-difference) checked with 0.1 s frame strips over the whole reel and per-frame differences around doubtful spots. 40 auto shots -> 41: added cuts hidden by a whip-pan (3.40), a car-body wipe (10.55) and a whip at 29.50; folded a 3-frame punch-in (21.20) into the last montage shot; merged motion-blur false cuts in the live-action tag (39.77-40.68 room, 40.12, 40.23). Long auto shots 2.17-7.02 and 21.20-26.10 checked by eye: the first hides one whip cut, the second is one continuous orbit plus a fade. 17.97 is a real jump cut (same framing, new clip). Audio: per-0.25 s loudness, band energies, spectral centroid and onset density with numpy (no listening possible).
