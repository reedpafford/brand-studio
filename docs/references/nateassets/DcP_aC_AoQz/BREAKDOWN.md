## Why it works

- The hook puts a number on the stakes: an empty wallet and '$2,000' in the first 3 s, against a named doubter ('MOM SAID I'D FAIL').
- Seven shots in under 5 s (door burst, wide reveal, wallet slam, chair spin, two whip reveals) - the energy of the edit sells the argument before the story starts.
- Rapid ping-pong dialogue (2-4 word lines at 3+ words/s) with single-word captions plays like a sitcom; the 'Sales: 3' phone insert is the punchline.
- A clear contract with a deadline ('One week... every dollar') gives the viewer a scoreboard to watch for the rest of the reel.
- A real low point (head on arms, 'I can't let her down') before the montage makes the win feel earned and keeps it about the relationship, not just money.
- Time and scale are shown visually: clock time-lapse through night and sunrise, stacks rising, an empty office filling as he counts '10, 20, 30'.
- The second montage turns Mom from opponent into partner, with match-cut world swaps and a cheek kiss - the emotional payoff most viewers share.
- 'We did it' - 'You did' - 'No, we did' gives a warm resolution, then the closet reveal and screaming ECU flip it into a laugh for the button.
- The live-action tag opens by copying the cartoon's last pose with a real person, tying the fable to real life ('based on a true story') before the lifestyle flex and loop.
