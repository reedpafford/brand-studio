## Structure
0-8%: Lamp on, the teller mid-gesture: a hero with the worst case of the problem in all the land. The child, flat: that isn't a real story. 8-35%: The story world: the tale's hero suffers the problem in outsized, funny ways. Back to the bed for the child's practical heckles; the teller patches the tale each time. 35-55%: Every fix the child suggested is tried in the tale and fails grandly. The child is sitting up now. 55-75%: A humble traveller hands over the magic thing. It works; a whole kingdom breathes out. And then what? Then the hero slept. 75-90%: The child spots the real one in the room and looks from it to the teller: the hero is you. A caught pause, then goodnight. 90-100%: Lights out. From the dark the child asks who the dragon was, gets no answer, then reads the product's name off the label.
