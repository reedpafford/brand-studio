## Settings
Movie style, Dialogue format: a photoreal short drama played straight, lines spoken on camera. About 105-135 seconds, four scenes. 9:16, no captions.

## The product in the story
A plant and a payoff. From about 10% it is in frame and never mentioned: in the hero's bag, on their bench, label partly seen. At about 60% the hero sets it on the table, label toward the accuser, names it and uses it there and then (an app: the phone turned to face the accuser). Named twice: by the hero as proof, and by the rival at the end.

## Structure
0-5%: The accuser puts the hero's result down between them: nobody gets this honestly. The hero's face, held still. 5-30%: How it came to this: the result praised, a rival's sour look, a complaint made. The product sits in frame, unremarked. 30-58%: A plain denial isn't enough, and the hero is benched, docked or sent home on the spot. A friend says: just show them. Why the hero never said (pride, or it seemed too simple to mention) comes out in one line. 58-82%: The hero returns, sets the product down and does it again in front of them, start to finish, hands never out of shot. Hold on the accuser's face as it works. 82-100%: A short, stiff apology. Then the rival, alone with the hero, asks what it is called. Their call to action, if any, follows the hero's answer, naming the brand; else end on the rival writing the name down. These shares hold at any length.

## Cast
All adults. Hero: the buyers' age and sex, exact, proud, slow to explain. Accuser: in charge, by the book, not cruel. Rival: a peer who complained, envious rather than wicked. Friend: blunt, loyal.

## Tone, sound and look
Dry and procedural: clipped questions, shorter answers; the quiet joke is how simple the truth is. A hushed room: paper, a chair, the product at work. Flat cool light for the accusation, window daylight for the return.

## Details you invent
The result and why it looks impossible, who can punish and how, the hero's reason for silence: each made from the product, who buys it, where it is used and what goes wrong without it, not the first stock choice (an exam hall, a courtroom).
