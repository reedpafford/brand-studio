## Settings
Movie style, Dialogue format: a photoreal short drama for a product that keeps people safe, lines spoken on camera. About 80-105 seconds, three scenes: the instant, that morning, afterwards. 9:16, no captions.

## The product in the story
Enters at about 15%, that morning: the hero fits, packs, checks or switches it on, label readable, while being teased for it. At about 60% it does its one job on camera, exactly as the person's words say it works and no more (an app: the alert on the phone in the hero's hand). Named twice: by the hero that morning, and by the doubter at the end.

## Structure
0-7%: The instant before. An ordinary moment about to go wrong: a sound, a head turning. Cut away before anything lands. 7-45%: That morning. The hero does the careful thing with the product; the doubter teases them, fondly, for worrying too much; the hero does it anyway. 45-70%: The instant again, played through. The danger arrives and the product does its job. Nobody is hurt: show the cause, the product working and the stillness after. 70-90%: Quiet. The doubter sits down where they are, hands not quite steady. Neither speaks for a long beat. 90-100%: The doubter picks the product up, reads its name aloud and takes the morning's teasing back in one line. Their call to action, if any, is the hero's reply, naming the brand; else end on the two of them, safe. These shares hold at any length.

## Cast
All adults, and nobody is injured. Hero: the buyers' age and sex, the careful one, teased for it. Doubter: a partner or workmate, easygoing, loved.

## Tone, sound and look
Easy everyday talk in the morning; almost no words afterwards. The day's own sounds, one sharp sound at the instant, then near silence: breathing, a road far off. Warm morning light, flat daylight for the instant, low sun after. A long locked hold on the quiet.

## Details you invent
The ordinary danger this product truly exists for, the morning's teasing, the doubter's last line: each made from the product, who buys it, where it is used and what goes wrong without it, never the first stock choice. Truthful, never gory, never a child at risk, never a claim the person didn't make.
