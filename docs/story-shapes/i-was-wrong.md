## The person's words
Their words and attachments override this card; the rest follows it. A pasted script stays word for word: the card then guides only staging, pacing and where the product lands. Only their own claims, offer, tagline and call to action; invent none. No product named: say so and ask, never invent a real brand.

## Settings
UGC style, Presenter format: one ordinary person talks to their phone in their own room, from the first frame to the last, with literal B-roll cut in. About 60-90 seconds, one talker, one room. 9:16, no captions.

## The product in the story
Enters at about 30%, in B-roll: still in its packaging on a shelf, pushed on them by someone who had had enough. Later in the talker's own hand at its real size, label readable, in use (an app: unopened on the home screen, then open in their hand). At their talking spot they only talk and hold nothing. Named three times: with scorn, plainly, then with respect.

## Structure
0-7%: To the lens, a little defensive: they told everyone this was a waste of money. 7-30%: Their three objections, as they really made them; B-roll of them being smug about each. 30-45%: How it got into the house anyway, and the day the old way let them down once too often. 45-75%: Objection by objection, what actually happened, only as far as the person's claims go. One small grumble may stand: it makes the turnaround believable. 75-90%: The apology owed: who they have to tell they were right, and that person's face, enjoying it. 90-100%: The longest hold on the talker: the verdict in one sentence. Their call to action, if any, is the last sentence, naming the brand. These shares hold at any length.

## Cast
The talker: the buyers' age and sex, opinionated, funny at their own expense. In B-roll only: the one who made them try it. Nobody else speaks.

## Tone, sound and look
Blunt and quick, laughing at themselves; sentences run on, one real stop before the verdict. The room's own quiet under the voice. The talker fills about half the video, still, eyes on the lens. B-roll: one simple action a shot, grey light for the old way, warm for the new.

## Details you invent
The three objections (the ones real buyers raise), the old way they swore by, who pushed it on them, the grumble that stands: each made from the product, who buys it, where it is used and what goes wrong without it, never the first stock choice.
