## The person's words
Their words and attachments override this card; the rest follows it. A pasted script stays word for word: the card then guides only staging, pacing and where the product lands. Only their own claims, offer, tagline and call to action; invent none. No product named: say so and ask, never invent a real brand.

## Settings
Zack D style and format: one calm narrator explains one surprising thing as a chain of cause and effect, while one continuous camera follows it through one real set in realistic game-engine 3D. Nobody speaks on camera. About 30-45 seconds. 9:16, no captions.

## The product in the story
The hook belongs to the surprising fact, not the product. The product enters at about 55% as the next link in the chain, the part that interrupts the cause: photo-accurate, on a surface or in a hand in the set, label readable, a cutaway or glow showing what it does, in the person's claims only (an app: on a phone lying in the set). Named once, or twice if their call to action names it.

## Structure
0-10%: Already happening on the first frame; the voice states the surprising fact as the camera catches it. Offer three opening pictures to choose from. 10-55%: The chain, in the order it physically happens: one clause, one picture of the part doing the work, the camera following the moving thing inside. A red glow marks the harm. 55-80%: The product joins the chain and the same journey carries on through it; blue or cyan for it working. 80-95%: Back out to human scale: the result, and one reaction face. 95-100%: The payoff line and one small visual gag within a second of the last word. Their call to action, if any, is that last line, naming the brand. These shares hold at any length.

## Cast
One ordinary person in the set, the buyers' age and sex, who reacts and never speaks. The narrator, voice only. A pet or bystander if the gag needs one.

## Tone, sound and look
Short, plain, factual sentences, about three words a second with no silences, slightly deadpan; never ad copy. Few sounds, each on its action. No hard cuts: places change on a fast move inside the world or a dissolve matched on the subject.

## Details you invent
The surprising fact (true and checkable), the set, the moving thing the camera follows, the closing gag: each made from the product, who buys it, where it is used and what goes wrong without it, never the first stock choice. Invent no fact about the product itself.
