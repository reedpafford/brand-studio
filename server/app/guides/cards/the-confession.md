## The person's words
Their words and attachments override this card; the rest follows it. A pasted script stays word for word: the card then guides only staging, pacing and where the product lands. Only their own claims, offer, tagline and call to action; invent none. No product named: say so and ask, never invent a real brand.

## Settings
UGC style, Presenter format: one ordinary person talks to their phone in their own room, from the first frame to the last, with literal B-roll cut in. About 50-75 seconds, one talker, one room. 9:16, no captions.

## The product in the story
Enters at about 55%, in B-roll: in the talker's own hand at its real size, label readable, then in use at home (an app: on the phone in their hand). At their talking spot they only talk and hold nothing. Named twice: when they found it, and in the close.

## Structure
0-8%: Face to the lens, first word on the first frame: they have never told anyone they do this. One sentence names the hiding ritual. 8-35%: The ritual in detail, the more exact the better: what, where, for how long, what it cost them. B-roll shows each step as it is said. One detail is funny; one stings. 35-55%: The day it stopped being bearable. Something small: someone nearly noticed. 55-78%: What they found, in the person's claims and the talker's own plain words; B-roll of the product in the hand and in use. 78-92%: Life without the ritual: one concrete freedom, shown. 92-100%: Back on the face for the longest hold: a line to whoever is still hiding it. Their call to action, if any, is the last sentence, naming the brand. These shares hold at any length.

## Cast
The talker: the buyers' age and sex, ordinary, a little embarrassed, then relieved. In B-roll only: the one who nearly noticed. Nobody else speaks.

## Tone, sound and look
Low and confiding, sentences running into one another like someone telling a friend; never an ad read. The room's own quiet under the voice. The talker fills about half the video, still, eyes on the lens. B-roll: one slow, simple action a shot, flat grey light for the hiding, warm for the after.

## Details you invent
The ritual and its exact steps (the true, slightly odd habit), the near miss, the one freedom, the room that says who this person is: each made from the product, who buys it, where it is used and what goes wrong without it, never the first stock choice.
