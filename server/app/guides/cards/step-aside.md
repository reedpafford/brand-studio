## Structure
0-8%: The problem, drawn enormous, is already winning and fills the frame. A big, confident character squares up to it and tells everyone to stand back. 8-25%: Round one, brute force: flung clear. 25-40%: Round two, a bigger character with a bigger gadget: worse. Each time the small hero raises a finger to speak and is talked over. 40-58%: Round three, everyone together: the biggest wreck yet, and the problem has grown. Silence. The small hero clears their throat. 58-75%: The small hero walks up alone, uses the product, and the giant thing shrinks to its true, ordinary size. 75-92%: The big ones, dishevelled, stare: it was there the whole time? The hero did say excuse me. 92-100%: The hero names it. Their call to action, if any, follows, naming the brand. The button: a big one needs both hands to lift the tiny product. These shares hold at any length.

## Cast
The small hero: an adult of the buyers' age and sex, the shortest and softest-spoken in the room, never in a hurry. Two big ones: loud, certain, kind underneath. The problem itself: huge, with no face.

## Tone, sound and look
The big ones shout in bursts of two to five words; the hero never raises their voice. Crashes, one long creak, the product's small click. Low angles under the problem, a high angle on the hero's walk; cool shadow turning warm as it shrinks.
