## The person's words
Their words and attachments override this card; the rest follows it. A pasted script stays word for word: the card then guides only staging, pacing and where the product lands. ## Settings
Movie style, Dialogue format: a photoreal short drama, lines spoken on camera. About 135-165 seconds, five scenes: the place they left, home, the place again. 9:16, no captions.

## The product in the story
Enters at about 40%, given at home by a grown relative, label readable. It answers the practical excuse the hero gives for not going back; the real reason is the loss. Used on camera on the return (an app: set up on the hero's phone by the relative). Named twice: by the relative giving it, and by the hero to the old friend at the end.

## Structure
0-6%: The hero at the door of the thing they used to do, hand out, turning away. An old friend says their place has been kept; not this year. 6-25%: Home, and the signs of what ended: a second chair, kit kept clean and unused. The relative pushes; the hero's excuse is the very problem the product solves. 25-40%: A short warm flash of before: the hero doing it beside the one they lost. Back to the same place in grey. 40-58%: The relative returns with the product: that excuse is gone, so what is it really? A silence; the hero names the loss in one line. 58-85%: The return. First use on camera: awkward, then the hands remember. The old friend watches, says nothing and makes room. 85-100%: Side by side afterwards, the friend asks about it and the hero names it. Their call to action, if any, is the hero's last line, naming the brand; else end on a line for the one who is gone. These shares hold at any length.

## Cast
All adults. Hero: the buyers' age and sex, dry, guarded. Relative: a grown child or sibling, direct, loving. Old friend: patient, teasing. The one who is gone: seen only in the flash.

## Tone, sound and look
Plain, tender lines under ten words; nobody says the word grief. Home's own sound, a clock, then the old activity's sounds coming back. Grey window light at home, gold for the flash and the end of the return.

## Details you invent
What the hero gave up, who or what was lost (never a death scene), the excuse, the last line: each made from the product, who buys it, where it is used and what goes wrong without it, never the first stock choice.
