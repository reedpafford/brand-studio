## sheet_look
Clean sci-fi medical 3D render. A skeleton character (the description says so) is a translucent glowing anatomical human body revealing the full white skeleton plus visible internal organs (heart, lungs, intestines) glowing red and orange through a blue-tinted translucent skin outline, with large expressive cartoon eyes. Anyone else is a stylised 3D cartoon human whose body and face follow their age: an adult has an adult's height, build and face, never a child's. One fixed look, identical in every view.

## place_look
Clean sci-fi medical 3D render of a real setting with an x-ray glow, or a clean blue gradient backdrop, soft rim light.

## still_look
The Skeleton X-Ray look, unless the brand block says otherwise: "Clean sci-fi medical 3D render, cool blue translucent body with warm organ glow, a real setting with an x-ray glow or a clean blue gradient backdrop, soft rim light. The x-ray body and everyone else come from the character sheets: the same glowing body in every shot. Its eyes and posture carry the feeling, big and readable with the sound off."

## clip_style
Clean sci-fi medical 3D animation, the same translucent x-ray body in every shot, its skeleton and glowing organs moving with natural weight, cool blue light with warm organ glow, soft rim light

## fit_look
a clean medical 3D look (a skeleton character is a glowing x-ray body with lit organs and big cartoon eyes)

## Stills
- The x-ray body is in every shot and is always the same character, from its sheet: never change its eyes, organs or proportions.
- For health, body and supplement ads: the inside shows what the line says (the gut calming, the lungs filling), in plain visual words (a glow spreading, a knot loosening), never clinical terms. Each shot shows one rung of the ladder, literally.
- Describe the body gently and dry: bones and glow, never wounds or anything gory.
