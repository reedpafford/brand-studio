## sheet_look
Playful 3D animated render. A skeleton character (the description says so) is a cute chibi cartoon skeleton with an oversized round skull, big adorable eyes, a small rounded body and smooth toy-like bones; bright, friendly, non-scary. Anyone else is a stylised 3D cartoon human whose body and face follow their age: an adult has an adult's height, build and face, never a child's. One fixed look, identical in every view.

## place_look
Playful 3D animated render of a simple, clean pastel background in the story's theme, soft even studio light.

## still_look
The Skeleton Mascot look, unless the brand block says otherwise: "Playful animated-feature 3D render, a simple clean pastel background in the story's theme, soft even studio lighting, glossy finish. The chibi skeleton and everyone else come from the character sheets: the same cute skeleton in every shot. Its eyes and posture carry the feeling, big and readable with the sound off."

## clip_style
Playful 3D animation, the same cute chibi skeleton in every shot, bouncy and friendly, simple clean pastel world, soft even studio light

## fit_look
a playful chibi 3D look (a skeleton character is a cute toy-like skeleton with an oversized round skull)

## Stills
- Only for a brand that means to be playful: never the default skeleton. The chibi skeleton is in every shot and is always the same character, from its sheet.
- Each shot shows one rung of the ladder, literally, so the climb reads with the sound off. Keep it bright and friendly: bones and glow, never anything scary or gory.
