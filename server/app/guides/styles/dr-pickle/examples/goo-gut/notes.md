## The style pattern
- **Host:** the same clay/vinyl-toy doctor as pickle-1 (peach skin, big nose, dark brows, slicked dark hair,
  white lab coat, light blue shirt, red-pink tie, brown trousers). Here he is larger and cut at the thighs, and
  he switches sides: centre-right for the first 11 shots, then mostly bottom-left from shot 12 on, so he never
  covers the action. He never cuts with the background.
- **Background character:** a fat body drowned in glossy yellow-orange goo, with a skull face, bulging eyes and a
  red-and-white striped sweatband, in navy briefs. Its belly opens as a window onto the organs. When it "heals"
  the goo drips away to show red muscle and bone, then a lean skin-covered man with the same skull face.
- **Places:** a royal-blue studio backdrop (top #0041a0, lower #3c69a6) for the explanations; warm real places

- **Contrast device:** the "warning" beats are desaturated black-and-white with grain; the "fixed" beats burst
  with light and colour.
- **Colour:** average about #6d5d56, luma 108, saturation 37 (the most saturated of the six: goo yellow
  #d0b34a against royal blue).

## Why it works (look and feel)
- The goo body is disgusting and funny at once; every claim about the gut is shown inside the body as it's said.
- The host steps to whichever side is empty, so the gross picture always reads.
