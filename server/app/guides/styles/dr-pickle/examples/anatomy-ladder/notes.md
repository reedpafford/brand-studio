## The style pattern
- **Two layers.** A full-frame generated background (cuts every ~2 s) and, composited over it, one small 3D host
  who never cuts: his pose and lip-sync run continuously across background cuts (checked frame by frame at 10.6
  and 14.1 s).
- **The host.** A clay/vinyl-toy-look 3D doctor: matte peach skin, long head, big rounded nose, prominent ears,
  thick dark slanted brows, small round white eyes with dark pupils, wide mouth with visible teeth, dark brown hair
  combed back over a high forehead. White knee-length lab coat with notched lapels, two hip pockets and a breast
  pocket; light blue collared shirt; red tie; brown trousers; brown lace-up shoes. Chunky sausage fingers.
  Full body, standing, bottom-right corner, about 37 % of frame height and 25 % of width, feet touching the
  bottom edge. Coat measured #8f7867 in shade (reads white); trousers #695d4e. Clean hard matte, no shadow on the
  background, no colour match to it.
- **What the host does.** Lip-syncs every word; blinks; small head bobs; one-hand gestures: an open palm toward
  the background ("look at this"), finger up, hand on chin, hand on belly, both palms up. He reacts with his face
  (eyebrows up, mouth wide) on the punchlines. He never walks or leaves the corner.
- **The background ("the green screen").** Photoreal-leaning 3D medical-illustration renders, animated (image to
  video). One recurring background character: an anatomy man, a flayed-muscle body (red-brown muscle fibre,
  ivory tendons) with a bare skull face that has cartoon eyeballs, wearing a red sweatband; sometimes a layer of
  yellow fat or skin over the muscle. He lives his life in real places (bathroom mirror, couch, gym, office,
  supplement aisle, front door) and on a blue studio backdrop (#27689f top to #1a4c7c bottom). Cutaways go inside
  the body: macro tissue, glowing particles, vessels, microbes, a brain with a nerve to the gut. Things happen as
  they are said: fat melts, skin opens, eyes glow, fog clears, a belly lights up.
- **Colour.** Average #72605e (1 fps), luma 114/255, saturation low-mid (20 on signalstats' scale). Warm
  amber interiors (warmth +19 to +30) alternate with cool blue studio and inside-body shots (−5 to +5). Skin and
  muscle tones carry most of the saturation.

## Why it works (look and feel)
- The corner doctor is a constant anchor: the background can jump anywhere every two seconds and the viewer
  still knows who is talking.
- The background shows the line literally and at once, so the claim is "proved" on screen as it is said.
- No music and almost no pauses: the blunt voice is the only sound, bleeps land like slaps, and the captions let
  you read the swear the bleep hides.

## How the director recreates the look
- Keep: a continuous host layer in a fixed corner; backgrounds that act out each line literally, from anatomy
  bodies to inside-the-body macros; ~2 s background shots with slow push/pull; a recurring anatomy-body figure;
  voice only; bleeped strongest swears with the word printed in the caption.
- Don't copy: the brand's pack, the day labels' exact rungs, the price and courier beats (that's this ad's
  offer), the specific red sweatband (it is this video's figure, though three of six videos use a sweatband).
