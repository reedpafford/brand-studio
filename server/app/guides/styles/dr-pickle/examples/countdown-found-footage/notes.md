## The style pattern
- **Host.** A polished, feature-animation-like 3D doctor (smooth skin shading, detailed short curly hair and a trimmed beard,
  big glossy eyes): a young Black man in an open white lab coat over a light blue button-down shirt, no tie.
  Medium shot, waist up, lower centre-right, about 35–40 % of frame height, cut by the bottom edge. Lively hands
  (finger counts, palms up, hand on chest, a wave), big facial reactions (eyebrows up, pursed lips, frowns).
- **Backgrounds.** A collage: real phone UGC of people with the symptom, real endoscopy and microscope footage,
  and AI 3D medical renders (gut lining, worms, cells, x-ray bodies). Gross-out close-ups are the point.
- **Countdown cards.** The host alone, full frame, on pure black, with a big number: a listicle spine.
- **Colour.** Average about #766360 (1 fps), luma 121, saturation low (14). Warm found footage, sickly yellow-green
  endoscopy, cool blue/green 3D renders.

## Why it works (look and feel)
- Disgust plus a friendly doctor: the gross real footage hooks, the cartoon doctor makes it watchable.
- The countdown cards give a sense of progress through a long video.

## How the director recreates the look
- Carries over: the doctor composited in front of every kind of footage; countdown cards for list scripts; a
  non-stop blunt voice with swears captioned in full.
- Differs from the folder's main look (5 of 6 videos): host design, italic captions, found footage instead of a
  recurring anatomy figure, music bed, no bleeps. Treat as a variant (an open question for Reed on the PR).
- Don't copy: real people's found clips (we can't use them), the brand's pack, the refund page.
