## sheet_look
Stop-motion clay puppet: a clay character detailed with tiny real craft materials — thread-wrapped hair, fabric scraps, wire accents, button details — with visible fingerprints and thumb smudges on the clay and chunky rounded clay hands with no fingernails. The body and face follow the character's age: an adult has an adult's height, build and face, never a child's; a teenager looks like a teenager, a child like a child. One fixed outfit of real fabric scraps with stitched seams, identical in every view, and one silhouette-defining item that reads at a glance.

## place_look
Stop-motion clay puppet: a fully sculpted clay set with tiny craft-material props, cinematic stop-motion film lighting with gentle shadows, a moody palette of deep teal shadows and warm amber practical light, real cast shadows.

## still_look
The Clay Puppet look, unless the brand block says otherwise: "Stop-motion clay puppet scene — clay characters detailed with tiny craft materials: thread-wrapped hair, fabric details, wire accents, on a fully sculpted clay set, cinematic stop-motion film lighting with gentle shadows, the scene filling the entire frame with the camera inside the puppet world. Faces and builds come from the character sheets, and outfits too unless this video gives its own. Deep teal shadows and warm amber practical light; no photographic skin, no real human hands."

## clip_style
Handmade claymation throughout: matte plasticine, visible fingerprints and tool marks, gentle smooth motion with one action per shot, a calm camera. Every cartoon touch is sculpted in clay. Non CGI. Animation must start at the first frame

## fit_look
a stop-motion clay puppet look with thread-wrapped hair and fabric details

## Stills
- Moody but never dark: deep teal shadows against warm amber practicals keep the clay reading; a face always has warm light on it.
- Name the craft materials on each character and prop (the thread hair, the wire-rimmed glasses, the button eyes on a toy). Name the clay on every sculpted object; glass and metal are "sculpted as a professional clay imitation of …"; liquids are glossy and wet against matte clay. The product is its real packaging, set on a clay plinth or held in clay hands, its label exactly as in its image.
- Never call the clay polished or glossy. Keep the camera inside the puppet world at a named angle; never show the set's edges or the workbench. Name the feeling, then the brows, lids and mouth.

## Clips
- One gesture and one camera move per shot: stacked actions drift. Repeat the craft and clay words in the action, so the material survives the motion.
- Clay can turn into the next shot: the scene kneads and reshapes into the next, or a clay smear wipes the frame. Name the one visible action that carries one shot into the next.
