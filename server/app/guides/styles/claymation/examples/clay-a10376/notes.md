## Look
- Two clay worlds:
  - **People (Cinematic lane):** rounded, appealing faces; dark eyes with clear whites and wet highlights, sculpted brows, blush on the cheeks; tight coiled clay noodle hair; a real chunky knitted jumper and a striped tee; tears modelled as glossy drops. Fine fingerprint-like striations across the skin in close-up (shots 11–12).
  - **The creatures (Goofy-lane faces):** a brain as a ball of coiled clay ridges with stubby arms, big cream googly eyes with sculpted lids; a crab-like creature that's mostly lips and teeth. Every surface shows dense fine parallel striations, like fingerprint whorls, the strongest "made by hand" cue in the video.
- Sets: a real-looking family room (sofa, lamp, bookshelf, wooden blocks) and a cave-like pink clay room with a pendant lamp, a door and two eye-shaped windows. Tidy, few props.
- Light and colour: the head starts cool and muted (pink walls, blue-white window light), average #826571, saturation 29–36. At the turn it shifts to warm gold (shots 11–12: #a26a47 then #845c3e, saturation 55–60, warmth 27–35). The room's colour change carries the story's change from stuck to working.
- Depth of field: shallow, with soft backgrounds in every close-up.

## The style pattern
- Cinematic clay people for the real world, and goofy clay creatures for the world inside them: the style holds two clay registers in one video and moves between them by cutting or flying through a window.
- Story beats are told by light: cool and muted while stuck, warm gold once it works.
- Tidy symmetrical miniature sets, shallow focus, slow pushes, long held wides for the comedy.

## Why it works
- The clay creatures make an abstract inner state concrete and funny; the cut back to a soft, warm human face lands the feeling.
- Long held wides let the comic timing play; close-ups punch each line.
- The light change is a reward you feel before you're told.

## How the director recreates this from a new script
- If the script has an inner or hidden world (a body, a mind, a machine), give it its own goofy clay creatures on a small tidy set, and keep the people in the Cinematic lane.
- Hold the wides 5–8 s; close-ups 2–5 s; save one fly-through or push-through for the turn.
- Shift the light from cool and muted to warm gold at the turn.
- Don't copy: the brain and crab characters, the eye-window room, the tablet, the end card.
