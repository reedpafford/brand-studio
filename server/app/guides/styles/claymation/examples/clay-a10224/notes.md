## Look

A blue-and-tan plaid flannel shirt that reads as **real woven fabric** (visible weave and texture), tan trousers, a leather belt. Chunky clay hands with clear sculpted fingers and short nails. In close-ups his skin is matte clay with pores and tool texture; in some wides the look slides to a smoother 3D animation *(a consistency slip)*.

- **Sets:** a lived-in, warm home (wood-panelled bathroom, armchair, bed, kitchen), a car, a clinic; plus black voids and warm brown backdrops for the diagrams.

Cold, grey light only for the low moments (kitchen table, saturation 19). The turnaround is the brightest and sunniest part.

- A warm, cosy Cinematic clay world around one relatable, imperfect man, cut against clay anatomy cutaways and **diagrams built as sculpted clay props** (checklists, split-screen dioramas, X marks, arrows, factories inside organs).

## Why it works
- The five-panel collage hook promises several things to see at once and previews the whole video.
- Clay makes uncomfortable body topics (spots, a big belly, organs) friendly and watchable.
- Sculpted infographics keep the explaining physical and on-style instead of switching to flat motion graphics.

## How the director recreates this from a new script
- For a list-style explainer: open with a collage of the coming shots; give each list item one human shot and one clay-diagram shot.
