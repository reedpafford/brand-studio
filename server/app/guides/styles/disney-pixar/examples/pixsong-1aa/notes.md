## The format pattern (shape only)
The pet sings: the symptom at home (trembling legs, can't jump on the bed) → people dismiss it → an old friend who got better → the owner finds it on her phone and orders → delivery and first chew → weeks pass, the joint glows → the bed jump and the hug, held through the instrumental → each ingredient as a journey to where it comes from → end card hug with the product.

## The style pattern
Disney-Pixar look in dark, firelit amber; an older animal lead with a tired face; ground-level rushing camera; X-ray glowing joints; ingredient journeys to magical places joined by zoom-blur whips.

## Why it works (for a viewer)
- A low camera behind trembling legs and a backward look: an owner's guilt in two seconds.
- The friend in glasses running after a ball sets up the turn as a funny visual.
- The bed jump is the payoff shot the whole song points at; the hug is given room in an instrumental.
- Ingredient journeys turn a list into a sequence of beautiful places.

## How the director recreates this from a new script
- Hook on the symptom from the sufferer's height (a ground-level camera), with the worried human soft in the background.
- Introduce proof as a character (an older friend who's already better) doing the thing the lead can't.
- Show the mechanism as glowing X-ray anatomy with light streaming in; mark time with flipping calendars.
- Give each ingredient its own short journey to a magical origin, joined by zoom-blur whips; time one whip to the music's re-entry after a break.
- Hold the emotional payoff through an instrumental stretch.
