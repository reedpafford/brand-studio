## Why it works
- A shocking act on frame 1, close and clean on a plain background, so the eye has only one thing to read.
- Each "because" gets a quick acted scene, then returns to the cyan home: the viewer always knows when we're explaining versus showing.
- Coloured glows tell the viewer where to look at phone size.

## How the director recreates this from a new script
- Open on the act itself, close, on the cyan home backdrop.
- Each reason is a short acted scene in a real-looking place painted in the world's cyan/teal; return to cyan for the abstract or summary beats.
- Link scenes with a subject-matched dissolve (0.15–0.35 s) or a fast camera move; keep every scene's camera moving and following the subject.
- Show time passing with an in-world object (a calendar, a growth), not a title.
- Don't copy: the dogs, the vet, the guard-dog story, the "ZACK D" lettering.
