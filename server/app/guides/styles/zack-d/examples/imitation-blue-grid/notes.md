## Format pattern
"This is what would happen if…" setup → the body change shown → the social change (people treat her differently) → emotional turn → why other things failed → the ingredients → social proof → offer. The Zack D cause-and-effect chain is there; the subject is a person's transformation rather than a mechanism.

## Style pattern
Pixar-cute 3D characters (big eyes, smooth skin, stylised curves) on an infinite blue grid cove (sampled #34679b at the top band; average frame #58677e; cool, warmth −13 to −26 on the kit's scale on blue-world shots). Props appear only as the line needs (bed, mirror, rack, desk, sofa). Glowing blue X-ray overlays for "inside". One recurring heroine whose outfit changes by scene.

## What it shows about Zack D
- It is the Starpop "Zack D" look (Pixar-cute on an infinite blue world, prop islands, X-ray glows) and matches today's `styles/zack-d` guide well.
- But it cuts every 1–1.5 s, has no music bed, a faster voice, and all-caps word-by-word captions: on every measured camera and sound trait it differs from the three originals. The Starpop skill itself warned that the cut rate is what makes imitations read as imitations.
- Don't copy: the body-change premise, the product, the ingredient mascots, the Facebook end card.
