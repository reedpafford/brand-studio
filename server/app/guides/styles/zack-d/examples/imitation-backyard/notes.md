## Format pattern
Mid-action open → mechanism (how it works, by following the scent) → the payoff for the viewer → proof over time (day 3, a week) → durability claim with a clock → social proof → offer → call to action. The first half is the Zack D format; the second half is a direct-response ad.

## Style pattern
Stylised 3D (Pixar-like faces and cartoon mosquitoes) mixed with photo-real product, real-looking backyards, and real phone footage at the end. The blue grid backdrop appears only in the opening seconds. Warm golden-hour grade: average #897553, warmth +13 to +45 on the kit's scale, saturation high.

## What it shows about Zack D
- Carries over: the mid-action open, voice from frame 1, a narrator at about 3 words a second with short pauses, the "follow the thing" camera for the mechanism (the scent trail), glowing coloured trails as explanation.
- Doesn't: the continuous camera (it cuts every 2.7 s after the first 10 s), one look (it mixes Pixar, photo-real and phone footage), the low plain bed.
- Don't copy: the product, the mascot, the offer and the end card.
