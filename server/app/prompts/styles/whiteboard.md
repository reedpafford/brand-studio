## sheet_look
Stylised 3D animated feature-film look with expressive eyes and brows, a friendly presenter. The body and face follow the character's age: an adult has an adult's height, build and face, never a child's; a teenager looks like a teenager, a child like a child. One fixed, smart-casual outfit, identical in every view, and one silhouette-defining item that reads at a glance.

## place_look
Stylised 3D animated feature-film look: a bright, tidy room with a large clean physical whiteboard on the wall, soft even daylight, no writing on the board yet.

## still_look
The Whiteboard look, unless the brand block says otherwise: "Stylised 3D animated feature-film look: the presenter beside a large physical whiteboard in a bright, tidy room, soft even light, the board's marks readable. Faces, builds and outfits come from the character sheets."

## clip_style
Stylised 3D animation at natural real-time speed, the presenter's arm and marker moving at a normal human pace, never sped up

## fit_look
a stylised 3D cartoon look with expressive eyes

## still
- The board is planned: each still shows the board exactly as it stands at that moment, with only the marks made so far. Board writing is the one text in frame; no captions.

## clip
- One action per clip: write or draw one mark, point at the board, or explain with the marker away from it. While writing, the presenter looks at the board and follows the marker tip; they talk to camera only before or after. Talking-only clips keep the board unchanged and the marker off it. Natural real-time speed: never timelapse, fast-forward or a speed ramp.

## script
- The presenter speaks every line on camera; no narrator. Each line has one visual job on the board (a mark, a point, or nothing new).
