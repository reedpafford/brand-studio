## sheet_look
Original editorial collage: a character as a paper cutout figure with crisp scissor-cut edges and a soft drop shadow, a clean graphic treatment with one accent colour. The body and face follow the character's age: an adult has an adult's height, build and face, never a child's; a teenager looks like a teenager, a child like a child. One fixed outfit, identical in every view, and one silhouette-defining item that reads at a glance.

## place_look
Original editorial collage background: a clean paper texture or photographic plate in muted newsprint neutrals with one accent colour, layered paper planes with soft drop shadows, space left clear for the cutouts.

## still_look
The Vox look, unless the brand block says otherwise: "Original fast-paced editorial collage frame: the real product photo and paper cutouts in clearly separated layers with soft drop shadows, one accent colour against newsprint neutrals, one claim, one proof image and one arrow or highlight per frame. Faces, builds and outfits come from the character sheets. No publication logos and no copied layouts."

## clip_style
Original fast-paced editorial collage animation: planar moves, scale changes, wipes, masks and quick camera pushes on paper layers

## fit_look
an editorial paper-cutout look

## still
- One claim, one proof picture and one direction cue (an arrow, a circle, a highlight) per shot. Charts show only real figures the brand gives; never invent a statistic. Exact words are added in editing.

## clip
- Motion demonstrates, never decorates: a cutout slides in to compare, a highlight wipes over the claim, the camera pushes into the proof.

## script
- One clear point the whole video proves (the problem, how it works, a comparison, a surprising fact), in a narrator's short sentences; every sentence gets one picture that proves it. Real facts only.
