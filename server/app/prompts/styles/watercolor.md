## sheet_look
Original watercolour illustration painted on cold-press paper: translucent layered pigment, visible paper tooth, softly irregular painted edges, restrained ink accents for the features, preserved white paper around the figure, a controlled palette. No glossy 3D surfaces and no flat vector gradients. The body and face follow the character's age: an adult has an adult's height, build and face, never a child's; a teenager looks like a teenager, a child like a child. One fixed outfit in one clear colour family, identical in every view, and one silhouette-defining item that reads at a glance.

## place_look
Original watercolour painting on cold-press paper: translucent layered washes, controlled wet-on-wet blooms, natural pigment granulation, softly irregular edges, restrained ink accents and preserved negative space, one controlled colour family for the place.

## still_look
The Watercolor look, unless the brand block says otherwise: "Original watercolour animation frame painted on cold-press paper: translucent layered pigment, visible paper tooth, controlled wet-on-wet blooms, softly irregular painted edges, restrained ink accents, preserved negative space and natural pigment granulation. Faces, builds and outfits come from the character sheets. No glossy 3D surfaces and no flat vector gradients."

## clip_style
Original watercolour animation, painterly motion only: translucent pigment, soft edges that move gently, recognisable forms held steady

## fit_look
an original watercolour painted look with soft ink accents

## still
- One controlled colour family per shot, so the washes never turn muddy. The product's shape and label stay legible, painted cleanly.

## clip
- Animate gently: the forms stay recognisable and only the soft pigment edges move. Turns can use the medium (a bloom spreading into the next place, a wash wiping across, a brush stroke revealing the product), to show the selling idea, never as constant decoration.
