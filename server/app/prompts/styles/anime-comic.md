## sheet_look
Original cel-shaded anime illustration: clean, even ink line weight, two-tone cel shading with one shadow tone, flat bright colour fills, a readable silhouette and simple facial proportions with large expressive eyes. An original character: never one that resembles an existing anime, manga or comic character, costume or franchise. The body and face follow the character's age: an adult has an adult's height, build and face, never a child's; a teenager looks like a teenager, a child like a child. One fixed outfit with simple, bold shapes, identical in every view, and one silhouette-defining item that reads at a glance.

## place_look
Original cel-shaded anime background art: clean ink lines, flat colour fills with one shadow tone, soft painted sky gradients, a clear readable layout with the depth carried by colour and value.

## still_look
The Anime comic look, unless the brand block says otherwise: "Original cel-shaded anime frame: clean, even ink lines, two-tone cel shading, flat bright colour, painted background art, a strong key pose and a readable silhouette. Faces, builds and outfits come from the character sheets. Expressions are big and graphic, readable with the sound off."

## clip_style
Original cel-shaded anime animation, clean ink lines and two-tone shading, pose-to-pose with purposeful holds and snappy impact frames

## fit_look
an original cel-shaded anime look with clean ink lines

## still
- Every shot is a strong key pose. Speed lines, halftone or an impact frame only when they sell the moment (the win, the reveal), never as decoration. No speech balloons or captions: words are added in editing.
- Prioritise silhouette and expression over fine detail. The product keeps its real shape and label, drawn in the same line and shading.

## clip
- Animate pose to pose: hold the key pose, snap to the next on the beat, with a restrained camera move. A reveal or a power-up can land on an impact frame.
