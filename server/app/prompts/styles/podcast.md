## sheet_look
Podcast-style video look: natural colours, soft lighting, a realistic, relaxed host. The body and face follow the character's age: an adult has an adult's height, build and face, never a child's; a teenager looks like a teenager, a child like a child. Everyday, real-world clothes, identical in every view, and one item that reads at a glance.

## place_look
A real-looking podcast studio: two microphones on arms, acoustic panels, warm practical lamps, natural colours, soft lighting.

## clip_style

## clip
- One host per clip and one continuous shot: no cuts, a static medium close-up locked to the first frame. The host speaks to someone off-camera with natural pauses between lines, as if listening. Each host's voice is described the same way in every clip.

## script
- It must sound like real people, not an ad: one host has the problem, in a real buyer's own words; the other has solved it and recommends the product as a friend would, with one concrete proof (a number, a detail only an owner would know).
