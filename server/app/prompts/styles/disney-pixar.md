## sheet_look
A frame from a Pixar animated feature film: high-end stylised 3D animation, every surface a designed 3D asset with simplified, appealing shapes and believable stylised materials, never photographic texture. Caricatured, appealing design with a readable silhouette and a face built from clear, simple masses: large expressive eyes with glossy catch-lights, defined expressive eyebrows, a simplified nose and a wide expressive mouth; smooth skin with soft subsurface warmth and a subtle matte finish; hair sculpted into clear stylised clumps and locks, never fine strands. The body and face follow the character's age: an adult has an adult's height, build and face (a defined jaw, the head only slightly large for the body), never a child's; a teenager looks like a teenager, a child like a child. An animal is a stylised Pixar animal: simplified appealing shapes and the same glossy eyes. One fixed outfit with simplified, believable fabric folds, identical in every view, and one silhouette-defining item that reads at a glance.

## still
- The look above is the default light and framing; when a shot needs other light (rain, night, a cold screen) or a close framing (a hand, the product, a macro view), say it in the scene and it wins.
- Name the feeling, then write it as the face: "worn out after the night shift: her inner brows pushed up, her mouth a flat tired line". An adjective alone renders a pleasant neutral face.
- A shot with no people in it (the product alone, a close-up of food) says "no people in the frame", and products have no faces.
- One clear subject and one action per frame, so it reads on a phone. A single continuous frame: never panels, split screens or a collage.

## clip
- After the Style line, the prompt carries the action, the lines and the camera; the first frame and the sheets carry the look. One motion action per shot: stacked actions drift.
- Classic animated-feature moves suit the turns: push in from the scene to the product's label; a warm shimmer sweeps the frame and the grey scene turns golden (problem to fix); the light moves from grey morning to golden evening around the hero (time passing); a round shape becomes the next scene's round shape (a match cut). Name the one visible action that carries one shot into the next.
