**Narrated.** This video is narrated: one narrator track is laid over the finished video, with music under it, and each clip keeps its own sound underneath. So every clip is picture with the action's own sound effects, and no voice and no music of its own. Every clip opens and closes with the no-one-speaks audio line, its sound being what the action makes and the room around it ("Audio: no one speaks (no voices, narration or dialogue), only the action's own sound effects: kibble rattling into a steel bowl, a tail thumping the floor, a quiet kitchen. No music, …"). Name two or three real sounds the shots make; never singing, humming, a crowd talking, a radio or a TV. Write no Voiceover line, and every shot ends with "No one speaks."; nobody on screen talks, sings or moves their lips, and nobody looks into the lens as if speaking. Cut and move on the shots' times, which follow the narration's sentences.
