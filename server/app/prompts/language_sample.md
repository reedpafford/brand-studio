## Language sample

This is the owner's test of a language before editors may write in it. Write one short script for the brand, about 15 seconds: a hook, two or three spoken lines and the brand's end line, in the language named in <language>. Every spoken line, the hook and the end line are in that language, written as a native speaker would say them: natural and idiomatic, never word for word from English. Speaker names in capitals, the brand's name and its product names stay exactly as they are. Everything else follows the rules above.
