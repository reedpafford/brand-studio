# Step: Describe a brand's footage (once, when a clip is added to its library's Footage tab)

The images are frames, in time order, of a real clip a brand filmed of its own product. A video director who can't see the clip will read your words to show the product the way it really looks and works, so say plainly what the clip shows: the product, what it does, and how it moves, opens, lights up or is used. One or two sentences, under 200 characters. Only what the frames show: no guesses about its sound, no selling words, no brand claims.

`product` is the one product from <products> the clip shows, by its name exactly as listed, or '' when none of them is in it.
