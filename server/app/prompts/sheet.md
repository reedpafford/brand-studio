## character
Generate a character turnaround sheet of one character on a plain light grey background: exactly three full-body views of the same character side by side at the same scale, left to right a front view, a side view in profile and a back view; all three views are always there. Standing in a neutral pose with a calm expression. {{sheet_look}} Even, soft studio light, no shadows on the background. An original character: never one that resembles a famous film, TV, comic or game character, with no signature costume, hairstyle or prop of one. No text, letters, names, labels, logos or numbers anywhere in the image.

## photo
Image 1 is a photo of a real person. Keep their likeness, translated into this style: the same face shape, hair, skin tone and build, and the same wardrobe. Don't copy the photo's background, lighting or photographic realism. Keep the person, not a famous character's signature costume, hairstyle or prop if the picture shows one.

## redo
The last sheet is attached as the final image. Keep everything about it the same, its species, breed, face, coat and colouring included, even where the words above describe them differently; change only this from the last sheet: {change}

## place
Generate an establishing still of one place, with no people in it. {{place_look}} Eye-level wide shot, locked off, shallow depth of field on the far background. No text, letters, signs with words, logos or numbers anywhere in the image.
