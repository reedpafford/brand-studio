## character
Generate a character turnaround sheet of one character on a plain light grey background: the same character three times in a row at the same scale, full body front view, full body side view and full body back view, standing in a neutral pose with a calm expression. Stylised 3D animated feature-film look (Pixar/Disney-like CG): slightly enlarged head (about 6 heads tall), big glossy round eyes with one sharp catchlight, thick expressive brows, small nose and soft subsurface skin. One fixed outfit, simple, clean and real-world, with fine cloth detail, identical in every view, and one silhouette-defining item that reads at a glance. Even, soft studio light, no shadows on the background. No text, letters, names, labels, logos or numbers anywhere in the image.

## photo
Image 1 is a photo of a real person. Keep their likeness, translated into this style: the same face shape, hair, skin tone and build, and the same wardrobe. Don't copy the photo's background, lighting or photographic realism.

## place
Generate an establishing still of one place, with no people in it. Stylised 3D animated feature-film look (Pixar/Disney-like CG), dense and specific set dressing with photoreal props, motivated cinematic light: warm amber practicals or golden-hour sun against cool blue shadows, deep but not crushed blacks, no blown highlights. Eye-level wide shot, locked off, shallow depth of field on the far background. No text, letters, signs with words, logos or numbers anywhere in the image.
