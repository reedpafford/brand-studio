## character
Generate a character turnaround sheet of one character on a plain light grey background: exactly three full-body views of the same character side by side at the same scale, left to right a front view, a side view in profile and a back view; all three views are always there. Standing in a neutral pose with a calm expression. A four-legged animal stands on all fours in its kind's natural pose, not upright like a person or a toy, unless the words below say it stands or walks like a person; an animal's breed, coat colour and one distinctive marking (a white chest blaze, one folded ear) are the same in every view. {{sheet_look}} Even, soft studio light, no shadows on the background. An original character: never one that resembles a famous film, TV, comic or game character, with no signature costume, hairstyle or prop of one. No text, letters, names, labels, logos or numbers anywhere in the image.

## photo
Image 1 is a photo of a real person. Keep their likeness, translated into this style: the same face shape, hair, skin tone and build, and the same wardrobe. Don't copy the photo's background, lighting or photographic realism. Keep the person, not a famous character's signature costume, hairstyle or prop if the picture shows one.

## sheet
Image 1 is this character's approved sheet, drawn in another style. Keep who they are, translated into this style: the same face shape, hair, skin tone, age and build, the same wardrobe and the same signature items (jewellery, glasses, a hat, a tattoo). Copy who they are, never how they are drawn: their rendering, proportions-as-drawn and colours of light come from this style, not the sheet.

## redo
The last sheet is attached as the final image. Keep everything about it the same, its species, breed, face, coat and colouring included, even where the words above describe them differently; change only this from the last sheet: {change}

## place
Generate an establishing still of one place, with no people in it. {{place_look}} Eye-level wide shot, locked off, shallow depth of field on the far background. No text, letters, signs with words, logos or numbers anywhere in the image.

## seedream
A character reference sheet for a film: one fictional character shown three times on a seamless light grey studio background. On the right, a large three-quarter portrait from head to upper chest that clearly shows the face. On the left, the full body from the front; in the middle, the full body from behind. Neutral stance, arms relaxed at the sides, empty hands, a calm closed-mouth expression. The same face, hair and outfit in all three views; the two full-body views at the same scale, head to shoes visible with space around them. Soft, even, neutral studio light, low contrast, soft contact shadows, natural unretouched skin and fabric texture, photographed like a still from a modern TV drama. No text, no logos.
