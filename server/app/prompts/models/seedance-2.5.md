Planning for this model:
- One clip can hold a whole scene with its cuts, up to its longest length, with lip-synced lines. Shots run back to back with no gaps; too little in a shot and it improvises, too much and it over-cuts.
- It follows its reference pictures closely: up to eight people and things in a clip stay steady; more may not. People are attached in the order they first appear, so list `people` in that order.
- It can hear a voice sample, so a speaker's voice can carry from clip to clip. Still give each speaker their voice words.
- Words on signs and labels can come out misspelled: keep text to what a reference picture already shows.
- Water, waves or echo in a scene's sound can cause whistling: leave them out unless the story needs them.
