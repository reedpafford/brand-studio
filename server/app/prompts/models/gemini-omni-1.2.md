Planning for this model:
- It makes realistic footage with its own voices and sound, cuts included. Several shots in one clip work when each is timed; a clip that is one shot should be one continuous move.
- A clip carries pictures of at most two people. A scene with more puts the others in a wide where they are small, or in a shot of their own described in a few words, or splits at a natural beat so each clip has its two.
- It cannot hear a voice sample. A voice stays the same only if its words stay the same, so give each speaker few, exact voice words and keep one conversation inside one clip where the scene allows.
- It follows plain timing and plain direction. One action per shot; a long list of actions in one shot causes changes nobody asked for.
- It adds sound on its own. Say the room's sound; music is never asked for.
